How I Convinced Myself I Was A Sharp

The spreadsheet said I was up $340. My bank account said I was down $3,900.

It lied because I built it to. Not on purpose — I don't think — but with the kind of subtle craftsmanship you don't notice you're doing when your hands are the ones doing it. The spreadsheet tracked my bets. It had columns. It had color coding. It had a running P&L that updated when I punched in numbers manually every Sunday night, sitting at the same kitchen counter where I'd started this whole thing, drinking the same brand of coffee that had witnessed the original crime.
