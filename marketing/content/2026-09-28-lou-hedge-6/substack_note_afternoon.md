Rule one. If two venues disagree on a price, that is not a bug. It is the entire reason both venues exist. A book is a store. It marks up its inventory of outcomes so it makes money on volume. It doesn't care what the true probability is. It cares what price will bring balanced money in on both sides so the vig prints. A the books is a group of people trying to guess the true probability, some of whom know more than you, and none of whom are running a store. Those two things are supposed to disagree. They disagree by design. The disagreement is their business model, not your opportunity. If you find yourself thinking you have discovered a gap, you have not discovered a gap. You have discovered a market working correctly and you standing in front of it thinking it is broken.
